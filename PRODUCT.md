# Christiane Buchmann

**Assumptions labeled** — written from the redesign brief (Siena / A24 / Vela / Neue Montreal) without a live interview.

## Product

Portfolio site for Christiane Buchmann, BVK — Director of Photography (Hamburg / Frankfurt). Showcases narrative, music video, commercial, and documentary cinematography; drives agency/contact enquiries.

## Audience

Producers, directors, commissioners, and Above the Line agency contacts evaluating DoP fit from stills, trailers, and credits.

## Brand commitments (brief-pinned)

- Preview redesign references: [siena.film](https://siena.film/) (home scroll + ticket buttons + footer layout), [siena.film/films/savoy](https://siena.film/films/savoy) (project hero + trailer frame), [a24.raviklaassens.com/production/tony](https://a24.raviklaassens.com/production/tony) (lined credit blocks), [velaaprivateisland.com/villas](https://www.velaaprivateisland.com/villas) (portfolio hero + filters + search).
- Typeface: Neue Montreal (PP Neue Montreal).
- Live public theme stays unchanged until the client buys; redesign ships only under non-indexable `/preview/`.

## Constraints

- Static WordPress theme; projects from `inc/projects.php`.
- Existing custom theatre trailer player reused in preview.
- Noindex preview routes; excluded from sitemap.
