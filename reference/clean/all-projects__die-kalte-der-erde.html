DIE KÄLTE DER ERDE

Tatort Saarbrücken

A Saar-Palatinate soccer derby keeps all the emergency services in Saarbrücken on their toes, including Chief Inspectors Leo Hölzer, Adam Schürk, Esther Baumann and Pia Heinrich. After the game, a death is reported from the emergency room. It soon emerges that the man spent the “third half” at a field match. During their investigation, the officers come up against a wall of silence. Because as much as the hooligans are enemies among themselves, their biggest and common enemy is the police.
