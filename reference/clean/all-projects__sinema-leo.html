Sinema Leo

Screened in over 60 Filmfestivals | Received two awards

The projectionist Elisonguo Kiwia loves movies. Everbody thinks he is crazy, because Kiwia's only interest is to go out to the countryside and show films - for free! He not only feels like an projectionist, he really wants to reach his audience. A short portrait about the tanzanian projectionist Elisonguo Kiwia.

Tansznia | Documentary | 4 min | HD

Director & DoP: Christiane Buchmann Post Production: Frank Schumacher Production Company: Filmwiese Filmproduktion
