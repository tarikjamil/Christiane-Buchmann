FALSCHES SPIEL

EIN FALL FÜR ZWEI

Leo is arrested and taken into custody. He is accused of beating the Romanian forced prostitute Melina Dumitru to death. All of Benni's attempts to get his friend out of this awkward situation fail. In the end, it becomes clear that someone is doing everything in their power to put Leo in prison for life with perfectly falsified evidence! He has no other chance: he uses a court hearing to escape. Hunted by the entire Frankfurt police force, Leo and Benni investigate the background to the plot and its masterminds: four years earlier, Leo had played a decisive role in proving the murder of a prostitute to red-light kingpin Hendrik Leibold in an undercover operation with a statement from Melina Dumitru. Although the source of these tips remained a secret, Leibold, now terminally ill, found out who framed him at the time and sought revenge: he had Melina killed and framed Leo for the murder so that he, like Leibold himself, would be imprisoned! But it's only when Leo and Benni find out who else is involved in this plot that they manage to use a trick to prove everything to Leibold and put a stop to it...
